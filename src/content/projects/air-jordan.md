Full leather Air Jordan built by hand. The original silhouette was deconstructed for pattern reference, then rebuilt in leather from the sole up.

## Process

![Pressing and shaping leather panels at the workbench](/images/air-jordan-poster.png)

Each panel was individually cut, skived to thickness, and bonded before stitching. The build follows the original panel layout with hand-lasted construction.
