Birkenstocks built from the ground up using only authentic brand materials. The straps were cut from plaid fabric, fitted with original buckle hardware, and mounted to genuine Birkenstock cork footbeds.

## Components

![Disassembled Birkenstock components — plaid fabric straps, buckle hardware, and cork footbed soles laid out before assembly](/images/Birk.jpeg)

## Assembly

![Completed Birkenstocks on shoe lasts with plaid straps fitted and buckled](/images/birk2.jpeg)

The cork sole, suede liner, and rubber outsole are all original Birkenstock parts. Only the upper straps were custom-made.
