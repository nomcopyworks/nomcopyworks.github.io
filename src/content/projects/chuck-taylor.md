Brown leather Chuck Taylor sneakers, built entirely by hand. Pattern drafted from an original pair, then cut, stitched, and lasted using vegetable-tanned leather.

## Process

![Hand-stitching leather upper panels for the Chuck Taylor build](/images/chuck-taylor-poster.png)

The upper was assembled panel by panel with saddle-stitched seams. Each piece was cut from a single hide to keep the grain consistent across the shoe.
